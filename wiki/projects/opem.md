**Role:** Developer
**Org:** ECSIM

Models the performance of proton exchange membrane fuel cells. Combines static and dynamic
models predicting optimum PEMFC operating parameters. Accepts operating variables — anode and
cathode feed gas, pressure and composition, cell temperature, current density — as well as
cell parameters such as active area and membrane thickness.
