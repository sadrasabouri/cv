## Summary
Art does the "smart" placement of typed special characters and letters so that together they
form a visual shape spread over several lines of text. It is ASCII art, in other words, but
delivered as a library that a program can call.

The PSF Software Development Grant awarded in May 2024 funded two additions to the library:
multi-line output and custom fonts.
