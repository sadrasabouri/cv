## Summary
A large, cleaned, ready-to-use open-source Farsi text corpus: ~**130GB, 250 million paragraphs,
15 billion words**. Used to train the **first T5 models for Farsi**.

What distinguishes it as an artifact is that the cleaning is already done. The corpus ships
plug-and-play, so it can be used as it is. It is released openly,
hosted as a Hugging Face dataset at `SLPL/naab`, which is what makes it usable by people with
no connection to the lab that built it.
