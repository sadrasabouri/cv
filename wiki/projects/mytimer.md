## Summary
MyTimer is a simple but comprehensive timer for terminal users: you set it directly from the
command line, so people who spend most of the day in a shell do not have to leave the terminal
just to find a timer.

It sits with Nafas in the human-computer interaction group of Sadra's open-source work.

MyTimer is a Python package, with its source public on GitHub and releases distributed through
PyPI.
