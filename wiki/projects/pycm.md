## Summary
PyCM evaluates classifiers after the fact: it works from the predictions a model has already
made and supports most per-class and overall statistic parameters you might ask for. It is
built for the case where accuracy alone doesn't tell you anything useful and you need the long
list of metrics instead — data scientists who need a broad array of measures to accurately
evaluate a wide variety of classifiers, rather than a single headline number.
