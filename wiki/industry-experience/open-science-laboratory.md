**Dates:** 2019 – 2025
**Role:** Co-founder

Co-founded and led development of open-source scientific tooling. Across the portfolio,
projects have collectively gained **5,000+ GitHub stars and 20M+ downloads**, with several
adopted into larger ecosystems (Keras, TensorBoard, conda-forge).

## What the role covers

Being a co-founder here means wearing every hat the work requires:

- **Full-stack software development** — building and shipping the libraries themselves
- **Software engineering** — maintaining them long enough to matter, and getting them into
  ecosystems like conda-forge, Keras, and TensorBoard
- **Data science** — the ML tooling line: [[../projects/pycm]], [[../projects/pymilo]],
  [[../projects/memor]]
- **User research** — running studies on the tools and updating them on what the studies
  showed, most directly with [[../projects/nafas]]
- **Technical writing** — the reports that document the tools and make their reasoning
  inspectable, e.g. [[../publications/rater-agreement-metrics]]
