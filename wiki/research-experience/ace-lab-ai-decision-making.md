How LLM presentation style influences the decisions people make, across two studies:

1. **Rhetorical style** — 98 participants verified factual claims using LLM advice rendered in
   different rhetorical styles, showing how styling alone moves performance, confidence, and
   engagement → [[../publications/advice-styles-contemplation|Did You Think This Through?: Effect of Varying Advice Styles on Users' Performance and Contemplation]]
2. **Reflection prompting** — 36 participants played a research prototype of **Diplomacy**
   with and without reflection interventions →
   [[../publications/prompting-reflection|Prompting Reflection: Encouraging Users to Critically Evaluate AI Suggestions]]
