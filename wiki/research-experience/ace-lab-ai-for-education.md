How AI systems explain things to people with different levels of expertise, across three
efforts:

1. **ELI-Why** — a dataset of 13.4K "why" questions benchmarking whether LLMs tailor
   explanations to expertise level; GPT-4 matched intended level only 50% of the time →
   [[../publications/eli-why]]
2. **CodeQL for novices** — a system bridging LLMs with CodeQL so novice programmers can
   analyze large codebases; 3.7× accuracy gain, 32% time reduction →
   [[../publications/synthesizing-program-analyzers]]
3. **Social discourse around papers** — an online system that retrieves and labels relevant
   social media discussion to enrich how researchers read literature →
   [[../publications/beyond-the-page]]
