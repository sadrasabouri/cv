**Full title:** Experimental dataset of electrochemical efficiency of a Direct Borohydride Fuel
Cell (DBFC) with Pd/C, Pt/C and Pd decorated Ni–Co/rGO anode catalysts

**Authors:** Sarmin Hamidi, **Sadra Sabouri**, Sepand Haghighi, Kasra Askari
**Venue:** ChemRxiv, 2022

## Summary
A measurement release rather than a modelling paper: the record of impedance and polarization
tests run on a Direct Borohydride Fuel Cell. Three anode catalysts are covered — Pd/C, Pt/C, and
Pd decorated Ni–Co/rGO — so the same cell is characterised across all three.

Three quantities are recorded: cell voltage, power density, and resistance. Each is tracked
against the three variables the experiments sweep — the weight percent of sodium borohydride,
the applied voltage, and the anode catalyst loading. The values are read off the polarization
and impedance curves the tests produce, with those curves fitted to an equivalent circuit of the
cell.

The dataset is published as the ECSIM `dbfc-dataset` repository on GitHub, alongside the
ChemRxiv preprint.
