**Full title:** Experimental dataset of electrochemical efficiency of a Direct Borohydride Fuel
Cell (DBFC) with Pd/C, Pt/C and Pd decorated Ni–Co/rGO anode catalysts

**Authors:** S Hamidi, **S Sabouri**, S Haghighi, K Askari
**Venue:** ChemRxiv, 2022

## Summary
Impedance and polarization test data for a Direct Borohydride Fuel Cell across Pd/C, Pt/C, and
Pd-decorated Ni–Co/rGO anode catalysts. Voltage, power density, and resistance are tracked as
functions of sodium borohydride weight percent, applied voltage, and anode catalyst loading,
evaluated with an equivalent-circuit model of the cell.
