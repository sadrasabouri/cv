**Venue:** Water Resources Research (AGU), 2024

## Summary
Hydrology adopted ML widely but rarely examined how data heterogeneity and sample size affect
accuracy. Using ~**18,000 soil samples** from the USKSAT database with XGBoost and repeated
random subsets, the study found that learning and validation curves **did not plateau even at
large dataset sizes** — the field's assumptions about "enough data" don't hold.
