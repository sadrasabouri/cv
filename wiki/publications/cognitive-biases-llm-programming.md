**Venue:** IEEE/ACM International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE), 2026 — accepted

## Summary
An observational study built on watching 36 developers actually work. It found that **48.8% of
their programming actions were biased**, and that more than half of those biased actions came
out of an interaction with the AI rather than from the developer reasoning alone — the tool is
not a neutral bystander to the bias, it is where much of it originates.

What the study ends up with is a validated taxonomy of **15 kinds of cognitive bias, covering
90 individual biases**, specific to developer–LLM interaction, together with a set of concrete
things developers and the people building these tools can do about them.
