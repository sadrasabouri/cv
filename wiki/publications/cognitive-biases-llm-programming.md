**Authors:** X Zhou, Z Saghi, **S Sabouri**, R Pandita, M McGuire, S Chattopadhyay
**Venue:** IEEE/ACM International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE), 2026 — accepted

## Summary
An observational study of 36 developers found that **48.8% of programming actions are
biased**, with over half of those instances originating in AI-driven interactions. The paper
contributes a validated taxonomy of **15 cognitive bias categories covering 90 biases**
specific to developer–LLM interaction, plus mitigation strategies for developers and tool
builders.
