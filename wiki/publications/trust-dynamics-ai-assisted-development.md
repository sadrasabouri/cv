**Authors:** **S Sabouri**, P Eibl, X Zhou, M Ziyadi, N Medvidovic, L Lindemann, S Chattopadhyay
**Venue:** IEEE/ACM International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE), 2025 — accepted January 2025

## Summary
A mixed-method study — a survey of 29 developers plus observation of 10 — on how developers
define, evaluate, and evolve trust in AI-generated code. Comprehensibility and perceived
correctness drive trust decisions, but developers frequently revise them: only **52% of AI
suggestions are ultimately accepted**. Contributes four validated guidelines for developer–AI
collaboration.
