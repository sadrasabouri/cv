## Summary
Historical languages are hard to work with, and for several reasons at once: the orthography is
complicated, what survived of the written record survived only in fragments, and there is no
standard way to represent any of it digitally. Standard NLP tooling assumes none of those
problems.

ParsiPy is a Python toolkit for reading historical Persian in spite of them. It provides
modules for tokenization, lemmatization, part-of-speech tagging, phoneme-to-transliteration
conversion, and word embeddings.
