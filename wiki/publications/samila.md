**Authors:** **Sadra Sabouri**, Sepand Haghighi, Elena Masrour
**Venue:** arXiv, 2025

## Summary
Samila makes images by scattering many thousands of points, each one placed by a formula with
random parameters. No individual point carries the picture; the image is what the accumulated
placements add up to. The paper sets out that method and the generator built on it.

## Randomness and the seed
Because the whole process is random, an image cannot be obtained again unless its seed was
kept. The seed is what decides whether a given picture is recoverable at all.
