## Summary
Pahlavi (Middle Persian) barely exists in digital form, and a language with no data is a
language on its way out. That is the stake the paper works from: the missing resources are not
an inconvenience for researchers, they are the thing putting the language at risk.

PahGen is a framework that translates English into Pahlavi by pairing grammar-guided term
extraction with zero-shot LLM translation, so that the generated sentences come out correct
both syntactically and semantically. The framework was then used to produce a dataset of **360
parallel English–Pahlavi texts**, all of them validated by experts.
