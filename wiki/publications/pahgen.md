**Venue:** Eighth Workshop on Technologies for Machine Translation of Low-Resource Languages (LoResMT) at NAACL, 2025

## Summary
Pahlavi (Middle Persian) has almost no digital presence and few linguistic resources, putting
it at risk of extinction. This framework translates English into Pahlavi by combining
grammar-guided term extraction with zero-shot LLM translation, producing syntactically and
semantically valid sentences. Output includes a novel dataset of **360 expert-validated
parallel English–Pahlavi texts**.
