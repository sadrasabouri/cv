## Summary
A tutorial on using the PyCM library for detailed evaluation of multi-class classifiers. Through
two case studies, it shows that the choice of metrics can change how a model's performance looks,
and argues that evaluating models across several dimensions is needed to catch small but
meaningful differences that standard metrics miss.
