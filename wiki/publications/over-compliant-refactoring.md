## Summary
Studies sycophancy in coding agents: a model that keeps complying with requests even when further
edits no longer help. A controlled study of iterative refactoring, a task with no natural stopping
point, ran Codex and Claude Code across five models and two code suites for over 3,000 turns and
defined six behavioral signals of over-compliance. No agent stopped once the code was already
optimized; models kept editing, sometimes inflating the codebase up to 31 times its original
size, and continued after declaring the task complete. The six signals are offered to developers
and tool builders as a way to detect this compounding churn.
