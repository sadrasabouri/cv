**Venue:** ACM Conference on Intelligent User Interfaces (IUI) 2027 (accepted October 2026)

## Summary
How users audit and retain control over AI agents acting inside spreadsheets. The paper
introduces **Pista**, a spreadsheet AI agent that decomposes execution into auditable,
controllable actions. A formative study (N = 8) and a within-subjects evaluation (N = 16) found
that active participation improved users' task comprehension, their perception of the agent,
and their sense of ownership over the output. First-author work; co-authored with Sumit Gulwani,
connecting to the Microsoft PROSE internship.

The work was covered by USC Viterbi News in October 2026.
