**Venue:** ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology (UIST), 2025 — accepted

## Summary
A research prototype pairing paper reading with informal social media insight, using LLMs to
retrieve and label relevant discussions. A formative study with eight researchers explored
how to expand academic perspective without importing the cognitive overload and distraction
of social feeds.
