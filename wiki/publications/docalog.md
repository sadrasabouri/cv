**Venue:** Document-grounded Dialogue and Conversational Question Answering Workshop (DialDoc) at ACL, 2022

## Summary
The team's entry for the DialDoc-22 (MultiDoc2Dial) shared task, and the substance of Sadra's
BSc thesis. Three-stage pipeline: a document retriever, an answer span prediction model, and a
final span picker that selects the most likely answer among all predicted spans.
