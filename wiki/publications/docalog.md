**Authors:** Sayed Hesam Alavian, Ali Satvaty, **Sadra Sabouri**, Ehsaneddin Asgari, Hossein Sameti
**Venue:** Document-grounded Dialogue and Conversational Question Answering Workshop (DialDoc) at ACL, 2022

## Summary
The team's entry for the DialDoc-22 (MultiDoc2Dial) shared task, and the substance of Sadra's
BSc thesis. Docalog is a three-stage pipeline: first retrieve the document the answer should
come from, then predict the answer span inside it, then pick the most likely span out of
everything predicted.

## Why this one matters
This is the paper Sadra has a soft spot for. It was his first, and the first time he followed an
idea the whole way from a thesis topic to something with his name on it in workshop
proceedings. It is what pulled him into NLP, and into research and publishing at all —
everything else he has published came after it.
