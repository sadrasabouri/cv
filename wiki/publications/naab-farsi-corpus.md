## Summary
Lower-resource languages like Farsi need large training data, and at the time the work was done
there was even less of it around than there is now. naab is the answer to that: an open Farsi
corpus that is already cleaned and ready to use — roughly **130GB, 250 million paragraphs, and
15 billion words**. The "plug-and-play" in the title is the claim
being made: the cleaning has already been done, so the corpus can be used as it is.

The name comes from the Farsi word *naab*, which means pure and high-grade — which is what the
team was going for.
