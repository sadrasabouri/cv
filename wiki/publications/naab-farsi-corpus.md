**Venue:** Journal of Artificial Intelligence, Applications and Innovations (JAIAI), 2024

## Summary
A large, cleaned, ready-to-use open-source text corpus for Farsi — roughly **130GB, 250
million paragraphs, 15 billion words** — built to address the training-data scarcity that hits
lower-resource languages hardest (and did so even more acutely at the time). The name comes
from the Farsi word *naab*, meaning pure and high-grade.
