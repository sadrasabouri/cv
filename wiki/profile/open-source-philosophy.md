Open-sourcing NLP research produced breakthroughs like ChatGPT — but generative AI also makes
it cheap to produce convincing yet flawed content inside research communities. Sadra frames
this as a **Frankenstein-Trojan threat to scientific integrity**: work that looks assembled
from legitimate parts and arrives inside the gates unchallenged.

The response is to build scientific software that makes its own workings inspectable.
Reproducibility and transparency are treated as properties of the tooling, not of the paper.

Co-founded [OpenSciLab](https://github.com/openscilab/) with a group of friends to pursue
this.
